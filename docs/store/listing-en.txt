Sakanahen: Fish Kanji Quiz

Short description (max 80 characters)
How many Japanese fish kanji can you read? Spot fakes and beat the clock!

Full description
Tuna, sardine, cod… In Japanese, almost every fish has its own kanji, built on the fish radical 魚 (sakana-hen). You may have seen them printed on a sushi bar teacup. How many can you read?

Sakanahen Kanji Quiz is a 20-question, 4-choice quiz on fish kanji. Answer as many as you can, as fast as you can, and beat your best record.

■ QUESTIONS 1–8: SPOT THE FAKE
Which one is the real kanji for "tuna"?
The real 鮪 hides among look-alike fake kanji that don't actually exist. Look closely at the right-hand part and pick the real one.

■ QUESTIONS 9–20: HARD FISH KANJI
鰶, 鱵, 鰙… what do these mean?
Every choice is the name of a real fish or water creature, so you'll need both knowledge and good instincts.

■ 177 KANJI
• 40 familiar fish and 137 hard fish-radical kanji
• Look up all 177 kanji, with meanings and readings, in the Kanji list
• Review the questions you missed on the results screen

■ EASY TO PLAY
• A game takes just a few minutes
• Ranked by correct answers, then time
• Cheerful sound effects and music (can be turned off)
• Share your result with friends
• Missed a question? Watch an ad to retry it (up to 3 times per game)
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese

No Japanese needed: in English mode, choose the fish names in English. Become a fish kanji master!
